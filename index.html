Our Story

Hyderabad's Heart,
Melbourne's Table

Born from a love of authentic Hyderabadi cooking, Baba Biryani brings the real flavours of the Old City to Melbourne. Every pot we cook is a labour of love — marinated overnight, slow-cooked on dum, and finished with saffron and fried onions the traditional way.

No shortcuts. No compromises. Just the biryani your grandmother would recognise.

Traditional Dum cooking method — sealed & slow
Fresh ingredients sourced daily
Authentic Hyderabadi spice blends
Made in small batches — never mass produced
Vegetarian options always available
